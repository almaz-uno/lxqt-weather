== What and why

The author wants the humidity and the pressure on the panel (2026-10-02). The
widget asks Open-Meteo for the block `current_weather`, which has neither: the
code sets both to zero, and the tooltip, which hides zeros, never shows its
"Humidity" and "Pressure" lines. `README.md` promises both since the first
commit (constitution, open question "`README.md` diverges from the code").

Under the temperature the panel shows the weather description, "Overcast";
the author finds it redundant next to the weather glyph and wants the humidity
and the pressure there instead, the description kept in the tooltip.

On the way: `current_weather` gives the wind in km/h, and the tooltip labels
the number as m/s — 3.6 times the real speed (research, R2).

Unchanged: the location, its setting and its sources; the weather glyph and
the temperature; the update triggers; the middle-click map; the User-Agent.

== Behavior

. *The request* to Open-Meteo asks for the block `current` with
  `temperature_2m`, `relative_humidity_2m`, `surface_pressure`,
  `weather_code`, `wind_speed_10m`, `wind_direction_10m`, and
  `wind_speed_unit=ms`, instead of `current_weather`; the blocks `hourly` and
  `daily`, requested and never used, are dropped.
. *The line under the temperature* shows the relative humidity and the
  pressure at the surface in millimetres of mercury: `61% · 756 mmHg`. The
  description of the weather leaves the panel. The messages of a failed
  update — "Network timeout", "No connection", "Update failed" — keep this
  line, as now.
. *The tooltip*: `Humidity: 61%`, `Pressure: 756 mmHg`, `Wind: 2.7 m/s` — the
  speed in metres per second at last — and `Conditions:` with the
  description, as now.
. *The pressure* is the surface pressure, as Yandex Weather shows it — the
  map of the middle click — converted at 1 mmHg = 133.322387415 Pa and
  rounded to a whole millimetre.
. *The setting* `show_description` keeps its key and its default (`true`)
  and now switches the line of humidity and pressure; the dialogs call it
  "Show Humidity and Pressure". A panel where the line was switched off keeps
  it off.

== Choices made for the author

. *The surface pressure in mmHg*, not the sea-level pressure in hPa: it is
  what the weather services in Russian give, Yandex Weather among them, and
  what a barometer at the place reads. At Skolkovo, 188 m above the sea, the
  two differ by about 23 hPa (research, R1).
. *The key `show_description` reinterpreted rather than renamed.* A new key
  would be absent from every panel configuration, and `PluginSettings` would
  take its default — the line switched off by a user would come back on.

== Existing installations

No key is added, removed or renamed; `show_description` is reinterpreted (see
the choice above): it now switches the line of humidity and pressure, the line
of the description being gone. The location keys of specification 002 are
untouched. Version 0.3.0: new behavior.

== Constitution

* "Settings and user data": `show_description` — the line of humidity and
  pressure under the temperature.
* "External services": the weather — the block `current`, the wind in m/s.
* The open question "`README.md` diverges from the code": the humidity and
  the pressure are no longer among the divergences.
