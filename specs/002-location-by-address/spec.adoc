== What and why

The widget finds its location by the public IP address (ip-api.com). Behind a
VPN that is the VPN's exit: on ws1, 2026-10-01, the widget showed the weather
of Frankfurt am Main. The author works at a fixed place and wants its weather:
the location is set once, by an address, in the widget's settings.

The system location service, GeoClue, was considered first and set aside by
the author (2026-10-01): a fixed place does not need it.

Unchanged: the weather source and request, the update triggers (timer, left
click, network change), the existing settings keys, the User-Agent, the
fallback to Moscow when the IP location fails. The middle-click map and the
tooltip use the set location when there is one.

== Behavior

. *The field.* "General Settings" of the settings dialog gets a field
  "Location": empty, an address, or coordinates — two decimal numbers,
  latitude and longitude, separated by a comma or spaces (`55.6928, 37.3475`).
  Under the field the dialog shows the place the saved text stands for — its
  name and coordinates — or why nothing was found.
. *On OK:*
.. empty field — the location is found by the IP address, as in 0.1.x;
.. coordinates — used as they are, and the name is the coordinates;
.. any other text — one search at the geocoder (below). Found: the first
   result gives the coordinates and a short name — the `name` of the result,
   or the first part of its `display_name` when `name` is empty — and the
   dialog saves and closes. Not found, or the request failed: the dialog stays
   open with the reason, and nothing is saved. OK is disabled while the search
   runs; it gives up after 10 s.
+
Text unchanged since it was last saved is not searched again.
. *Stored* in the plugin's section of the panel configuration:
  `location` — the text as entered; `location_latitude`,
  `location_longitude`, `location_name` — what the widget uses. Clearing the
  field removes all four.
. *With a location set* the widget requests no location at all — nothing goes
  to ip-api.com — and asks Open-Meteo for the stored coordinates on every
  refresh. The tooltip says `Location: <name>`, the middle click opens the map
  at the stored coordinates.
. *The geocoder* is Nominatim of OpenStreetMap:
  `GET <geocoder_url>/search?q=<text>&format=jsonv2&limit=1`, with the
  User-Agent `lxqt-weather/<version>`. The key `geocoder_url`, default
  `https://nominatim.openstreetmap.org`, switches the service without a new
  build, as the Nominatim usage policy requires of client applications. A
  search happens only on OK — never as the text is typed — and at most once
  per OK.
. *Attribution*: the "Information" group of the dialog says that addresses
  are found by Nominatim, © OpenStreetMap contributors; `README.md` says the
  same.
. *`weather-test`* gets the same field in its "Settings" group, through the
  same code, so that the search can be checked without the panel;
  `weather-test --location <text>` fills the field and applies it at start.
  The search, the parsing and the keys live outside the plugin files
  (constitution, "Stack and layout": LXQt headers stay in
  `lxqtweatherplugin.h`); `IWeatherSettings` moves from
  `lxqtweatherwidget.h` to a header of its own, so that they need no widget,
  and gains the removal of a key.

== Choices made for the author

. *Nominatim, not the geocoder of Open-Meteo.* The author asked for an
  address; Open-Meteo finds populated places only (research, R2).
. *Searched once, on OK; the coordinates are stored.* A refresh makes no
  geocoder request — the usage policy asks for results to be cached, and a
  fixed place does not move.
. *Coordinates in the same field* — for an address OpenStreetMap does not know
  under the author's spelling (research, R1).
. *Four keys rather than one.* The coordinates are the location; the text only
  refills the field, and the name only labels the tooltip.

== Existing installations

New keys only. A panel configuration without them — every installation of
0.1.x — keeps finding the location by IP, as before; nothing is migrated.
Version 0.2.0: new behavior and new settings keys (constitution, "Releases and
versions").

== Constitution

* "Settings and user data": the keys `location`, `location_latitude`,
  `location_longitude`, `location_name`, `geocoder_url`.
* "External services": Nominatim — on OK of the settings dialog only;
  ip-api.com — only when no location is set.
* The open question "Location data": with a location set, the IP address is
  not sent to ip-api.com, and the address goes to Nominatim once, over HTTPS.

  at the cost of a second request; the name is the coordinates.
